Instructions

Objective

You're dropped in one of the 7 Wonders of the World in Google Street View and hear a siren beeping. (Also get a map of the siren's direction relative to yours in Radar mode.) Navigate to find the source of the siren. The siren itself is invisible, but when within 15m, it becomes constant; keep moving till the hunt is over. You get 7 minutes in each location. Hunt all 7 to complete the game.

Controls

Laptop/Desktop: Click the on-screen arrows to move forward/turn, or use 'Arrow keys'. To look around, click-n-drag. Switch between Sound and Radar mode by clicking on the icon or pressing 'Space'. Press 'Escape' to pause or quit.


Mobile: Tap on-screen arrows to move. Drag to look around. Tap the Sound or Radar icon to switch modes. To pause, tap the power icon.

Note

- The siren beeps faster as you get closer. So does the radar.
- Using siren earns more score than using radar.
